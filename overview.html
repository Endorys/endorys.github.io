ENDORYS.

Engineering deeper
tissue understanding.

An early-stage medical technology initiative exploring controlled ultrasound, conformal sensing and calibrated analysis.

Stage: concept development and synthetic software examples. Pre-prototype; physical and clinical validation remain ahead.

The engineering problem

Contact, geometry and acquisition conditions can change the signals a sensing system observes. Endorys investigates how to make those conditions explicit in a repeatable tissue-response measurement.

The product concept

  • Matrix: a proposed conformal sensing interface.
  • Acquisition: controlled acoustic excitation and response capture.
  • Insight: calibration-aware analysis with traceable metadata and uncertainty.

Application discovery

Breast tissue research is the original clinical motivation. Superficial tissue and procedural assessment are additional candidates. A first clinical indication has not been selected or validated.

No diagnostic accuracy, cancer detection, tomography, elasticity measurement or therapeutic benefit is claimed.

Development milestones

  1. Define a reviewable measurement protocol.
  2. Demonstrate a reproducible non-biological acoustic phantom baseline.
  3. Compare an integrated sensing module with the conventional reference.
  4. Define one medical intended use and its quality, risk, usability and regulatory pathway.
