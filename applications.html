APPLICATION DISCOVERY

Clinical questions.
Evidence before indications.

Breast tissue research is our original motivation. Further application candidates remain exploratory; a first clinical indication has not been selected or validated.

01 / ORIGINAL MOTIVATION

Breast tissue research

Explore whether calibrated acoustic observations can support repeatable tissue characterization.

Question to investigate
Can acquisition and geometry effects be separated from a reproducible sample response?
Evidence needed
Phantom evidence first; a future clinical study would need a defined intended use, expert oversight and relevant approvals.
02 / CANDIDATE DIRECTION

Superficial tissue research

Accessible sensing locations, including forearm soft tissue, are possible future settings for studying contact and repeatability.

Question to investigate
How sensitive is a measurement to changes in contact, placement and motion?
Evidence needed
A stable reference, sensing-depth assessment and confounder controls before any human study.
03 / LONGER-TERM CONCEPT

Procedural tissue assessment

Investigate whether localized sensing could add useful tissue context to an existing clinical workflow.

Question to investigate
What additional information would change one specific clinical decision?
Evidence needed
A validated sensing module, clinician-defined requirements and separate safety, workflow and clinical evaluations.
Research-stage programme. No validated medical device, clinical performance or therapeutic benefit is claimed. Application concepts guide research; they are not available products.

The initial experiment is non-biological. No cancer detection, screening, elasticity measurement, tomography, surgical guidance or therapeutic performance is claimed.
