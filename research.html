E001 · PHANTOM BASELINE

Acoustic Reference
Test

The planned experiment uses a conventional water-path setup and a non-biological sample to assess timing, placement and temperature effects on repeatability.

Review the protocol ↗
  1. 01 / CURRENT

    Review the experimental design

    Review the protocol, apparatus requirements, controls and acceptance criteria before data collection.

  2. 02 / NEXT

    Establish the acoustic reference

    Document acquisition conditions, raw-data provenance, repeatability, uncertainty and unsuccessful runs.

  3. 03 / CONDITIONAL

    Evaluate an integrated module

    Compare the proposed module with the conventional reference to determine whether it offers a measurable engineering benefit.

  4. 04 / LONGER TERM

    Define one medical intended use

    Define the clinical question, performance requirements and appropriate quality, risk, usability and regulatory development plan with expert input.
