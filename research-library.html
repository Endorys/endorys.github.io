01 / RESEARCH ARCHITECTURE

A connected architecture.
From sensing to analysis.

The measurement question: how can tissue response be characterized repeatably when contact, sensor geometry and acquisition conditions vary?

The proposed architecture combines a conformal sensing interface, controlled acoustic acquisition and external analysis. Sensor geometry, contact conditions and uncertainty are treated as part of the measurement record.

The immediate objective is a reproducible acoustic phantom experiment. An integrated sensing module would then be evaluated against that reference. Clinical usefulness requires separate investigation.

FOUNDING RESEARCH FOCUS

Breast tissue research

Investigate the measurement requirements for repeatable acoustic characterization of tissue response. Breast assessment is the original motivation for Endorys.

Research question
Can a consistent sample response be distinguished from changes in acquisition conditions and sensor geometry?
Evidence needed
A reproducible phantom baseline, followed by a defined intended use and an appropriately designed study with expert oversight and relevant approvals.
No screening, cancer detection or diagnostic performance is claimed.

The initial experiment uses a non-biological phantom. Human application illustrations describe future research concepts and do not establish clinical performance.

RESEARCH STATUS & PARTICIPATION
Is an Endorys device available for clinical use?

No. Endorys is a pre-prototype research programme. Platform and application illustrations describe proposed configurations. Clinical accuracy, therapeutic benefit and an available medical device have not been established.

Which tissues and applications are in scope?

The programme began with breast tissue assessment and expanded into tissue measurement and a separate materials-testing workstream. The immediate experiment uses a non-biological phantom and does not involve human testing.

Who can contribute to the research?

Acoustic measurement researchers, sensing engineers and clinicians who can contribute to a defined measurement question or intended use. Laboratory access and collaboration arrangements require separate agreement.

What does selective openness mean?

Selected protocols, documentation and reproducibility tools are public under explicit component licences. Future commercial engineering is reviewed separately before release. Existing public licence grants remain in force.
