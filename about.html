Built on engineering experience.

Experience in medical-device software, laboratory automation and hardware–software integration informs the Endorys approach. Sadhu worked at Siemens Healthineers from June 2008 to July 2025.

Endorys began with a breast-focused motivation and developed into a broader tissue research concept, previously called JUPITER. The task now is to turn that architecture into a bounded, reproducible engineering experiment.

Endorys is independent. It is not a Siemens Healthineers product, endorsed programme or affiliated venture.

Selective openness.

We share selected protocols, documentation and software examples for scrutiny. Future commercial engineering, partner material and new datasets require ownership, disclosure and licensing review before release. Existing public licence grants remain in force.
