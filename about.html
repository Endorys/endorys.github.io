ABOUT ENDORYS

A systems approach
to tissue measurement.

Endorys is an independent research and technology venture founded by Sadhu Shobab Bollipo in Bengaluru, India, with selectively open documentation and reproducibility tools.

Grounded in medical-device engineering.

Sadhu brings 17 years of experience at Siemens Healthineers, from June 2008 to July 2025. His work in medical-device software, laboratory automation and hardware–software integration informs the Endorys emphasis on system integration, traceable measurements and testable requirements.

Originally developed under the JUPITER name, Endorys began with a breast tissue assessment question and expanded into a broader sensing architecture. Current work concentrates on a defined acoustic experiment that can test the measurement assumptions before application development.

Endorys is independent. It is not a Siemens Healthineers product, endorsed programme or affiliated venture.

Selective openness.

Our intended commercial model is paid R&D and potential licensing of separately developed, rights-cleared technology. This direction depends on physical evidence and a defined partner need; no proprietary product or paid offering is established today.

Selected protocols, documentation and software examples are published for independent review. New commercial engineering, partner material and datasets undergo ownership, disclosure and licensing review before release. Existing public licence grants remain in force.

Development governed by evidence.

Endorys is at the pre-prototype stage. Physical and clinical validation remain outstanding; the public programme does not establish diagnostic accuracy, therapeutic benefit or regulatory approval. No investment or commercial partnership is implied.
