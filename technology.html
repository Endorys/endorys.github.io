THE ENGINEERING QUESTION

Separate the sample
from the setup.

Contact pressure, coupling, position and acquisition settings can change the observed signal. Our question is whether those effects can be tracked well enough to support a repeatable sample-response measurement.

Optional force, motion, impedance, thermal and optical sensing are research candidates. Their inclusion depends on calibration, spatial registration, interference testing and demonstrated incremental value.

The integrated matrix and its proposed benefits have not been demonstrated. Conventional acoustic measurements come first.
