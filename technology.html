THE ENGINEERING QUESTION

Distinguish sample response
from measurement conditions.

Contact pressure, acoustic coupling, sensor position and acquisition settings can influence the recorded signal. Endorys investigates how these factors can be characterized and controlled to support repeatable measurements of sample response.

Force, motion, bioimpedance, temperature and optical sensing are candidate additions. Each modality requires appropriate calibration, spatial registration, interference assessment and evidence that it contributes information beyond the acoustic measurement.

A conventional acoustic reference is the first validation step. The integrated matrix and any incremental measurement benefit remain to be demonstrated.
